About Me

Background

Where Healthcare Meets Technology

Tobby Akinwale is a final-year Business and Information Technology student at TU Dublin, Blanchardstown, with over 8 years of professional experience in social care and healthcare across Dublin.

She brings a rare combination — technical skills to build systems, and frontline healthcare experience to understand what those systems need to do. From trauma-informed care to TypeScript, from case management documentation to database design, she works fluently across both worlds.

She is the healthcare insider building healthcare technology — and that perspective changes everything about how the work gets done.

Her career path is not a pivot — it is an evolution. Every year in healthcare has sharpened her instincts for compliance, data integrity, person-centred design, and the cost of getting things wrong. These are exactly the instincts that produce better technology.
